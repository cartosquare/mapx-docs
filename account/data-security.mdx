When you upload data to MapX, **you control who can see it**. MapX is built around the principle that your spatial data — your customer locations, site surveys, operational data — belongs to you. This page explains how MapX stores your data, who can access it, and what you should consider when working with sensitive datasets.

## Data Storage

Your uploaded files (CSV, GeoJSON, Excel) and generated map projects are stored in **secure cloud storage** with encryption at rest and in transit.

- **Free, Basic, and Pro** plans use shared cloud infrastructure with logical tenant isolation — your data is kept strictly separate from other users' data.
- **Enterprise** plans include **dedicated storage** — your organization's data resides in an isolated environment, not shared with any other MapX tenant.

MapX retains your files and project data as long as your account is active. Enterprise plans can negotiate **custom data retention policies** based on compliance needs.

## Access Control

You control the visibility of every project independently:

- **Public projects** are visible to anyone who has the link — no MapX account required. Public projects are indexed by their link only; they are not listed in any public directory.
- **Private projects** (Pro and Enterprise) require the viewer to be authenticated and explicitly invited. Only collaborators you have added — or users with a valid private link you have generated — can access private projects.

You can change a project's visibility at any time in **Project Settings → Visibility**. Switching a project from public to private immediately revokes access for anyone who hasn't been explicitly invited.

## Data Sharing

**MapX does not share your uploaded data with third parties.** Specifically:

- Your files are not used to train AI models.
- AI analysis runs **in-session on your data** — prompts and results are scoped to your project and are not visible to other users.
- MapX does not sell, license, or otherwise transfer your data to external organizations.

## Sensitive Data Guidance

<Warning>
  Do not upload data containing **personal identifiers** — such as customer names, national ID numbers, phone numbers, email addresses, or financial records — into **public projects**. Public projects can be viewed by anyone with the link.

  Always use **private projects** (Pro or Enterprise) for datasets that include personally identifiable information (PII) or that are subject to data protection regulations (GDPR, HIPAA, CCPA, etc.).
</Warning>

If you are unsure whether your dataset contains sensitive information, err on the side of caution and set the project to private before uploading.

<Tip>
  Use private projects for any client or confidential data — it takes one click in project settings and ensures only invited collaborators can access your analysis.
</Tip>

## Enterprise Security

Organizations with stricter compliance requirements should consider the Enterprise plan, which includes:

- **SSO (SAML / OIDC)** — enforce authentication through your corporate identity provider
- **Admin controls and audit logs** — track data access and exports across your entire organization
- **Dedicated storage** — your data is fully isolated at the infrastructure level
- **Custom data retention policies** — configure how long MapX retains files and project history to match your internal or regulatory requirements
