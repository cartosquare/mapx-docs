## Choosing a Plan

- **Free** — best for exploring MapX, learning the interface, or running occasional small analyses. The 30-prompt monthly limit is enough for light experimentation.
- **Basic** — ideal for analysts doing regular work who need clean, watermark-free exports and a larger project library. 300 prompts / month, 30 projects, and 30 GB of storage cover most individual workloads.
- **Pro** — built for power users. 500 AI prompts per month, 300 projects, 50 GB of storage, project sharing to other members, project manifest export, and watermark-free high resolution static map PNG exports make this the right choice for professional spatial analysis work.
- **Business** — for teams of 2–5 who share one quota pool: 1000 AI prompts / month, unlimited projects, and 200 GB of storage across all members. The owner manages seats and can invite members by email.

<Tip>
  A single Pro seat at **\$89/month** covers what a traditional ArcGIS Desktop license costs at **\$600+ per year** — and MapX requires zero GIS training.
</Tip>

## Business (Team Plan)

Business is designed for small teams that share one account pool. The team owner purchases the plan and invites up to 4 members by email; everyone shares the monthly quota (AI prompts, storage, and projects). Each member keeps their own projects and data — leaving the team simply returns them to their personal plan.

All plans include core AI chat, auto-detected location columns, and CSV / GeoJSON / Excel uploads. For questions about your current plan, contact [support@mapxagent.com](mailto:support@mapxagent.com).
