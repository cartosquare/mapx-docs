## Choosing a Plan

- **Free** — best for exploring MapX, learning the interface, or running occasional small analyses. The 50-prompt monthly limit is generous enough for light experimentation.
- **Basic** — ideal for analysts doing regular work who need clean, watermark-free exports and a larger project library. The jump to 500 prompts / month handles most individual workloads.
- **Pro** — built for power users and small teams. Unlimited prompts, private sharing, custom export formats, and 5 collaborators per project make this the right choice for professional spatial analysis work.
- **Enterprise** — for organizations that need SSO, white-label branding, unlimited collaborator seats, and automated scheduled reporting. Pricing is custom based on usage and seat count.

<Tip>
  A single Pro seat at **\$49/month** covers what a traditional ArcGIS Desktop license costs at **\$600+ per year** — and MapX requires zero GIS training.
</Tip>

## Enterprise

Enterprise is designed for organizations with demanding data volumes, compliance requirements, or customer-facing mapping products. Enterprise includes:

- **Unlimited AI prompts and projects**
- **Dedicated cloud storage** — your data is isolated from other tenants
- **Unlimited team seats** — onboard your entire organization
- **SSO** (SAML / OIDC) for centralized identity management
- **White-label exports and reports** — ship MapX-powered analysis under your own brand
- **Scheduled auto-reporting** — automatically generate and deliver reports on a set cadence
- **Custom data retention policies** and admin audit logs

Pricing is custom and based on your organization's needs. To get a quote or schedule a demo, contact [support@mapxagent.com](mailto:support@mapxagent.com).
