MapX bills AI usage in credits. Every plan includes a monthly credit allowance plus storage, exports, and sharing features — you only pay more as your workload grows. See [Credits](/en/account/credits) for how billing works.

## Choosing a Plan

- **Free** — best for exploring MapX, learning the interface, or running occasional small analyses. 300 credits per month covers light experimentation.
- **Basic** — ideal for analysts doing regular work who need clean, watermark-free exports and a larger project library. 3,000 credits per month handles most individual workloads.
- **Pro** — built for power users and small teams. 12,000 credits per month, project sharing to other members, project manifest export, and watermark-free high-resolution static map PNG exports make this the right choice for professional spatial analysis work.
- **Business** — a team plan for up to 5 seats sharing one 150,000-credit pool per month, 1 TB of storage, and all Pro features.

<Tip>
  A single Pro seat at **\$99/month** covers what a traditional ArcGIS Desktop license costs at **\$600+ per year** — and MapX requires zero GIS training.
</Tip>

## Enterprise

Enterprise is designed for organizations with demanding data volumes, compliance requirements, or customer-facing mapping products. Enterprise includes:

- **Unlimited credits and projects**
- **Dedicated cloud storage** — your data is isolated from other tenants
- **Unlimited team seats** — onboard your entire organization
- **SSO** (SAML / OIDC) for centralized identity management
- **White-label exports and reports** — ship MapX-powered analysis under your own brand
- **Scheduled auto-reporting** — automatically generate and deliver reports on a set cadence
- **Custom data retention policies** and admin audit logs

Pricing is custom and based on your organization's needs. To get a quote or schedule a demo, contact [support@mapxagent.com](mailto:support@mapxagent.com).
