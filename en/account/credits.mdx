MapX charges AI usage in **credits**. Every AI turn consumes credits based on the actual tokens the model processes — not on a fixed per-question fee. Your plan includes a monthly credit allowance, and you can top up when you need more.

## Monthly credit allowance

| Plan | Monthly credits |
|---|---|
| Free | 300 |
| Basic | 3,000 |
| Pro | 12,000 |
| Business (team) | 150,000 shared by the owner and up to 4 members |

Unused allowance resets at the start of each billing month. Your plan's credit allowance is separate from any credits you top up.

## How a turn consumes credits

When you send a message, the AI processes three kinds of tokens, each priced differently:

| Token type | Price per 1M tokens |
|---|---|
| Input (uncached) | $0.445 |
| Cached input | $0.015 |
| Output | $1.334 |

The cost is converted to credits at **100 credits per USD** and rounded to the nearest whole credit:

```text
credits = (uncached input × $0.445 + cached input × $0.015 + output × $1.334) / 1M × 100
```

In practice this means:

- A short, direct question costs a few credits.
- Long analyses, large files, or long conversations cost more because they process more tokens.
- Reusing context from earlier in the conversation is cheaper — cached input tokens cost about 3% of uncached input.

You can see your allowance, usage, and balance on the **Plans** page in the dashboard. When your balance runs out, MapX pauses the chat and prompts you to top up.

## Top-up packages

Top-up credits never expire and are used after your monthly plan allowance:

| Package | Price |
|---|---|
| 1,000 credits | $10 |
| 5,000 credits | $50 |
| 10,000 credits | $95 |

## Business team pools

The Business plan is a team plan. The owner and up to 4 members share one credit pool of 150,000 credits per month. Every member's turns draw from the same pool, so usage across the team counts together.

<Info>
  Credit pricing and top-up packages are configuration constants on the server. They can change; this page reflects the current defaults.
</Info>
