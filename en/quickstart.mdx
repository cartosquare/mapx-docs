This guide walks you through the complete MapX workflow from a blank screen to a shareable interactive map. By the end you will have created a project, uploaded a dataset, run your first AI-powered spatial query, and exported the result — all in under a minute.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your free account">
    Go to [app.mapxagent.com](https://app.mapxagent.com) and sign up with your email or GitHub account. No credit card is required. You will land on your project dashboard immediately after confirming your email.

    ![Sign up with your email or GitHub account](/images/quickstart/sign-up.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new project">
    Click **New Project** and choose how to start:

    - **Blank project** — an empty workspace you build up from scratch.
    - **Sample project** — load one of the built-in sample datasets to explore MapX.
    - **Upload data** — pick local files (CSV, GeoJSON, Excel, etc.); a project is created and you land in the workspace.
    - **Import project** — restore a previously exported project bundle (`.zip` / `.mapx.zip`).

    You can add more data or adjust settings later from the project workspace.

    ![Choose a blank, sample, upload, or import project](/images/quickstart/new-project.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your data">
    Drag and drop a **CSV**, **GeoJSON**, or **Excel** file onto the upload zone, or click **Browse files** to select one. MapX will:
    1. Scan your column headers and automatically identify latitude/longitude or address columns.
    2. Show a preview of detected location columns for you to confirm or correct.
    3. Render your data points on the map canvas within seconds.

    Not sure what format to use? See [Uploading Data](/en/uploading-data) for full details on supported formats and column naming conventions.

    ![Upload a CSV, GeoJSON, or Excel file](/images/quickstart/upload-data.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask your first question">
    Click the **AI Chat** panel and type a question in plain English. A simple starting prompt is:

    > "Show locations on a map, color points by [column name]"

    MapX will interpret your request, apply the spatial operation, and update the map in real time. You can follow up with refinements like *"zoom to the top 20 by revenue"* or *"add a 1 km buffer around each point"* — the AI maintains context across the conversation.

    ![Type a spatial question in the AI chat](/images/quickstart/ask-question.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export or share your map">
    When you are happy with the result, choose one of the following:
    - **Download PNG** — exports a static image of the current map view.
    - **Generate Report** — produces a standalone HTML file with interactive maps, charts, and AI-written narrative insights. Click **Download** or **Copy share link** to distribute it.
    - **Copy share link** — anyone with the link can view the live interactive map. Your project stays private by default; you can revoke or disable sharing at any time.

    ![Share or export your map](/images/quickstart/share-export.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Example Prompts by Use Case

<CodeGroup>
  ```text Retail & Site Selection
  Show all stores within 500 m of metro stations, color by monthly revenue
  Score candidate sites by foot traffic, competitor distance, and median income
  Highlight any store location with revenue below $10,000 in red
  ```

  ```text Logistics & Delivery
  Draw 10-minute drive-time zones around each depot
  Find all delivery addresses more than 30 minutes from the nearest warehouse
  Cluster delivery stops into 5 zones and color each zone differently
  ```

  ```text Real Estate
  Show properties listed above $500,000 within 1 km of a school
  Overlay planning boundary polygons on top of the property points
  Color parcels by zoning category and show a legend
  ```

  ```text Environmental & Infrastructure
  Show all monitoring stations within 2 km of a river polygon
  Heatmap incident reports from the last 12 months
  Highlight sites that intersect with flood-risk zones
  ```
</CodeGroup>
