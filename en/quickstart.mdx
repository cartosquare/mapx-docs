This guide walks you through the complete MapX workflow from a blank screen to a polished, shareable result. By the end you will have created a project, uploaded a dataset, run your first AI-powered spatial query, and styled or shared the result — all in under a minute.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your free account">
    Go to [app.mapxagent.com](https://app.mapxagent.com) and sign up with your email or GitHub account. No credit card is required. You will land on your project dashboard immediately after confirming your email.

    ![Sign up with your email or GitHub account](/images/quickstart/sign-up.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new project">
    On the dashboard, open **New Project** and choose how to start:

    - **Blank project** — an empty workspace you build up from scratch.
    - **Sample data** — explore MapX with a built-in sample dataset.
    - **Upload files** — pick local files (CSV, Excel, GeoJSON, Shapefile, GeoTIFF, and more); a project is created and you land in the workspace.
    - **Import project** — restore a previously exported project bundle (`.zip` / `.mapx.zip`).

    You can add more data or adjust settings later from the project workspace.

    ![Choose a blank, sample-data, upload, or import project](/images/quickstart/new-project.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your data">
    Drag and drop files onto the upload area, or click **Upload Data** and select them. MapX supports **CSV, Excel, GeoJSON, Shapefile, and GeoTIFF** files, plus text, Word, and PDF documents that the AI can read for analysis.

    For table files, MapX scans the column headers, automatically detects latitude/longitude or address columns, and shows a preview so you can confirm the mapping before data is rendered on the map. Uploads run in the background — you can keep working while files convert, and paid plans support single files up to **20 GB**.

    Not sure what format to use? See [Uploading Data](/en/uploading-data) for the full list of supported formats and column naming conventions.

    ![Uploaded GeoJSON file rendered on the map with a completed upload queue](/images/quickstart/upload-data.jpg)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask your first question">
    In the **Chat** panel, type a question in plain English. A simple starting prompt is:

    > "Show locations on a map, color points by [column name]"

    MapX interprets your request, applies the right spatial operation, and updates the map in real time. Follow up to refine the result — *"zoom to the top 20 by revenue"* or *"add a 1 km buffer around each point"* — and the AI keeps the whole conversation in context. Type `/` to reference layers, files, data sources, and other assets directly.

    Learn how to get the most out of prompts in [AI Chat](/en/concepts/ai-chat), and see the supported operations in [Spatial Operations](/en/concepts/spatial-operations).

    ![Ask a spatial question in the AI chat and see store points colored by category](/images/quickstart/ask-question.jpg)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Polish and deliver the result">
    Once you are happy with the map, you can refine the presentation and hand it over:

    - **Style layers** — adjust colors, labels, transparency, and renderers through the chat or the [layer style editor](/en/concepts/layers).
    - **Switch 2D/3D and save your view** — turn points, lines, and polygons into 3D columns, elevated paths, or extruded surfaces with one click, then save the camera position. See [3D View](/en/concepts/3d-view).
    - **Add charts** — ask for a bar, line, or pie chart and pin it to the map as visual evidence. See [Charts](/en/concepts/charts).
    - **Generate a report** — create a self-contained HTML report with interactive maps, charts, and AI-written insights, preview it on a canvas, then download it or share a link. See [Reports](/en/concepts/reports).
    - **Share your work** — deliver report links, embed an interactive map on a website, generate a social-ready image, or open access to teammates. See [Sharing](/en/sharing/sharing-maps). You can also export a static PNG from the **Share** panel.

    ![Share panel with report, embed, social image, and project sharing options](/images/quickstart/share-export.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Example Prompts by Use Case

<CodeGroup>
  ```text Retail & Site Selection
  Show all stores within 500 m of metro stations, color by monthly revenue
  Score candidate sites by foot traffic, competitor distance, and median income
  Highlight any store location with revenue below $10,000 in red
  ```

  ```text Logistics & Delivery
  Draw 10-minute drive-time zones around each depot
  Find all delivery addresses more than 30 minutes from the nearest warehouse
  Cluster delivery stops into 5 zones and color each zone differently
  ```

  ```text Real Estate
  Show properties listed above $500,000 within 1 km of a school
  Overlay planning boundary polygons on top of the property points
  Color parcels by zoning category and show a legend
  ```

  ```text Environmental & Infrastructure
  Show all monitoring stations within 2 km of a river polygon
  Heatmap incident reports from the last 12 months
  Highlight sites that intersect with flood-risk zones
  ```
</CodeGroup>
