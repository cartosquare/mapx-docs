Charts turn your layer and file data into visual evidence. Instead of building
a chart by hand, describe it in chat — *"bar chart of population by city"* —
and MapX generates it from the selected data source.

## Create a chart

Ask for the chart you need in the chat panel. MapX supports bar, line, area,
pie, donut, and scatter charts. The generated chart appears in the **Charts**
section of the data panel.

Each chart includes:

- an AI-written insight that states the main takeaway in one sentence;
- the source layer or file and the fields it was built from;
- the row count behind the chart, so you know what it represents.

## Preview and export

Click a chart in the data panel to open a large preview. From the preview you
can:

- export the chart as PNG (2x), SVG, or CSV;
- rename or duplicate it;
- delete it;
- ask the AI to modify it in chat.

## Pin a chart to the map

You can pin one chart to the map at a time. The pinned card floats over the map,
can be dragged and resized, and is a convenient way to keep the evidence next
to the geography.

Click a category in a pinned or previewed chart to highlight the matching
features on the map. Click the same category again to clear the highlight.

## Charts in reports

Charts you create can be included in [reports](/en/concepts/reports). The
report embeds the chart with its title, insight, and data source note, so the
chart stays traceable to its underlying data.

<Note>
  Charts are snapshots taken when they are generated. To change the data or
  the visual, ask the AI to modify or regenerate the chart.
</Note>
