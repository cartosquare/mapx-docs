MapX lets you edit a layer's data and geometry without leaving the browser. Open
the attribute table from a layer in the Layers panel, inspect the rows, and make
changes that are written back to the layer's source. Editing works for layers
you uploaded as GeoJSON files and for PostGIS database layers alike.

Changes save immediately: each edit you finish is committed on the spot — there
is no separate "start editing" session to begin or end.

## Open the attribute table

In the Layers panel, find the layer you want to edit and click the table icon.
The table opens at the bottom of the workspace, showing one row per feature.
Click a row to highlight the corresponding feature on the map; click it again
to clear the highlight.

## Edit attributes

- **Edit a row** — click the **Edit feature** button on a row to enter its edit
  state. Only values you actually change are written back when you finish.
- **Edit a cell** — click a value, change it, then press Enter or click away.
  The change is saved immediately. Numbers and booleans are validated against
  the column type.
- **Add a field** — use the add-field control in the table header, choose a
  name, type (string, number, or boolean), and an optional default value.
- **Delete a field** — remove a column you no longer need. Fields that are used
  by the layer's style or labels cannot be deleted until you remove that use.
- **Delete a row** — click the row's delete button and confirm. The feature is
  removed from the layer.

## Edit geometry

Click the pencil button on a row to enter geometry editing. MapX shows the
feature's vertices on the map:

- **Move a vertex** — drag it to a new position; the shape previews live as you
  drag.
- **Add a vertex** — click a midpoint handle on a line or polygon edge.
- **Delete a vertex** — double-click a vertex (lines keep at least 2 points,
  polygon rings keep at least 3).
- **Move a point feature** — drag the point marker to its new location.

Use **Save** to commit the geometry, or **Cancel** to discard the changes. If
you try to leave the table, switch features, or delete the feature while a
geometry edit is still unsaved, MapX asks you to **Save**, **Discard**, or
**Cancel** first.

## Editing with others

If someone else changes the same feature while you are editing it, MapX keeps
the latest version and asks you to apply your change again — your edit is not
silently lost.

## Editing limits

Layers with up to 10,000 features and 20 MB are fully editable. Larger layers
open in read-only preview mode (up to 100,000 features and 200 MB) so you can
still browse rows and highlight features, but not modify them.

<Note>
  Every edit is saved as soon as you finish it, and there is no undo stack.
  Review your changes before confirming a delete.
</Note>
