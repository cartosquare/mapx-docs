MapX gives you four ways to share your work — report links, embeddable maps, social-ready images, and project access for other users. All sharing options live in the **Share** panel, opened from the project toolbar.

## Sharing Options at a Glance

| Type | What it shares | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Report Share** | A shareable link to an HTML report | Client deliverables, stakeholder updates |
| **Embed Share** | An interactive map embed link or iframe code | Websites, dashboards, portals |
| **Social Media Share** | A high-resolution map image | Social posts, presentations, documents |
| **Project Share** | Project access for specific users | Team collaboration |

## Report Share

After you generate an HTML report (see [Reports](/en/concepts/reports)), the **Share** panel lists your reports. For each report you can:

- **Generate a link** — creates a shareable URL for the report.
- **Copy or open the link** — send it by email or chat; recipients can open it in any browser without a MapX account.

Report links are a convenient way to deliver polished, interactive analysis documents instead of screenshot folders.

## Embed Share

The **Embed Share** section turns your project into an interactive map that can live on any website:

- **Enable embedding** — generates an embed URL protected by a token.
- **Copy the embed link or iframe code** — paste the URL anywhere, or copy the ready-made `<iframe>` snippet into your site's HTML.
- **Choose what viewers see** — toggle layer controls and the layer list on or off before copying.
- **Preview** — open the embed to verify how it looks.
- **Regenerate the token** — invalidates previously copied embed links.

Anyone with the embed link can view the interactive map in a browser — pan, zoom, and inspect data — without a MapX account. To take a project offline, disable embedding in the Share panel.

![Embed share panel](/images/sharing/embed-share.png)

## Social Media Share

The **Social Media Share** section generates a high-resolution map image you can post or insert anywhere:

- Choose an **aspect ratio** (3:2, 16:9, 4:3, 1:1, or automatic) for the target platform.
- Select a **resolution** (1x, 2x, or 3x) and preview the exact capture area on the map.
- Toggle the **watermark** — Free plans export with a MapX watermark; Basic and above can export without one.

The generated PNG downloads to your device and is ready for social posts, slide decks, or documents.

![Social media share panel](/images/sharing/social-share.png)

## Project Share

The **Project Share** section shares the project itself with specific users:

- Enter the recipient's **email address** to invite them to the project.
- Manage who has access from the same panel — you can **remove a user** at any time.

Project sharing is included with plans that support team collaboration. See [Plans](/en/account/plans) for availability.
