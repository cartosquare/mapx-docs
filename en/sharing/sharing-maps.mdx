MapX makes it easy to share spatial analysis with clients, teams, or the public — no GIS expertise required on the receiving end. Whether you're presenting findings to a stakeholder, collaborating with analysts, or embedding a live map in a dashboard, MapX gives you flexible, plan-appropriate sharing options.

## Public Sharing

Public link sharing is available on **all plans**, including Free. When you generate a public link, anyone who has it can view your map in a read-only browser window — no MapX account required.

- All data points are **fully rendered** in public shares — nothing is truncated or hidden from viewers.
- Public maps display your uploaded layers, styling, and any AI-generated analysis overlays exactly as you see them.
- To generate a public link, open your project and click **Share → Copy Public Link**.

<Note>
  All plans include public link sharing with full data rendering. You never need to upgrade just to share a map with a client or colleague.
</Note>

## Private Sharing

Private sharing is available on **Pro and Enterprise** plans. Private links require the recipient to authenticate before viewing — ensuring that sensitive or client-specific maps stay confidential.

With private sharing you can:

- **Control permissions** — choose whether the recipient can only view, or can view and edit the project.
- **Revoke access** at any time from your project's sharing settings.
- **Invite by email** — recipients are sent a direct link tied to their MapX account.

To generate a private link, open your project and click **Share → Invite People** or **Share → Generate Private Link**, then set the permission level (Viewer or Editor).

<Tip>
  Use private sharing for any client or confidential data. Public links are convenient, but private links ensure only the people you choose can access your analysis.
</Tip>

## Team Collaboration

MapX supports real-time collaborative projects for teams:

- **Pro plan** — add up to **5 collaborators** per project. Collaborators can view, annotate, run their own AI analysis prompts, and (if granted Editor access) modify layers and data.
- **Enterprise plan** — **unlimited seats** per project, with centralized user management and admin controls.

Collaborators receive an email invite and can join the project immediately after accepting. All collaborators see the same live project state — no version conflicts or manual file sharing needed.

For a full breakdown of what each collaborator role can do, see the [Team Collaboration guide](/en/account/team-collaboration).

## Embed Maps

**Pro and Enterprise** plans support embedding maps directly into websites, internal dashboards, or client portals via `<iframe>`.

To get your embed code, open your project and click **Share → Embed**. Copy the generated snippet and paste it into your site's HTML.

A basic embed looks like this:

```html
<iframe
  src="https://app.mapxagent.com/embed/project/YOUR_PROJECT_ID"
  width="100%"
  height="500"
  frameborder="0"
  allowfullscreen
  title="MapX Spatial Analysis">
</iframe>
```

Replace `YOUR_PROJECT_ID` with the ID shown in your project's share settings. Embedded maps are interactive — viewers can pan, zoom, and inspect data points without leaving your site.
