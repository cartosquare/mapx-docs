MapX supports multiple export formats designed for different downstream use cases — drop a PNG into a slide deck, hand off enriched GeoJSON to a GIS pipeline, or send a client a polished HTML report. The formats available to you depend on your plan.

## Export Options by Plan

| Export Type | Free | Basic | Pro | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| High resolution static map PNG export | ✓ (watermark) | ✓ (no watermark) | ✓ | ✓ |
| Online hosted report sharing | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| CSV / GeoJSON data download | ✗ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Project manifest export | — | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Scheduled exports | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ | ✓ |

<Note>
  Downloading your raw and analyzed data (CSV / GeoJSON) requires **Basic or higher**. Free users can view and analyze their data but cannot download it as CSV or GeoJSON.
</Note>

## Exporting a High Resolution Static Map PNG

High resolution static map PNG export captures the current state of your map canvas as a static image, including all visible layers, labels, and AI-generated overlays.

**Steps:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Export panel">
    With your project open, click the **Export** button in the top-right toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose PNG">
    Select **PNG Image** from the export format options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select resolution">
    Choose your desired output resolution. Higher resolutions are recommended for print or large-format displays.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download">
    Click **Download**. The file will save to your browser's default download location.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Free plan high resolution static map PNG exports include a **MapX watermark** in the bottom corner. Upgrade to Basic or higher to remove it.
</Note>

## Downloading Your Data

MapX doesn't just export your original upload — it exports your **analyzed and enriched data**. After running AI-powered analysis, new columns are often added to your dataset, such as:

- **Scores** (e.g., walkability index, risk scores)
- **Buffer results** (e.g., features within 500m of a point)
- **Joined attributes** (e.g., census data joined to your locations)

You can download this enriched dataset at any time as:

- **CSV** — for spreadsheet tools, BI platforms, or further processing
- **GeoJSON** — for GIS tools (QGIS, ArcGIS, Mapbox, etc.) that consume spatial formats

Each layer in the data panel has a **download button** on the right side of its row. Click it to download that layer's data as CSV or GeoJSON.

## Report Export

MapX can generate a one-click **HTML report** summarizing your map, key findings, and AI analysis narrative — ready to share with clients or embed in internal documentation.

Generated reports appear in the data panel under **Reports**. Each report row has an **export button** on the right — click it to download the HTML report file.

- **Free / Basic**: standard MapX-branded report template
- **Pro**: branded reports (custom title, logo, colors)
- **Enterprise**: fully white-labeled reports with no MapX branding

For a full walkthrough of the report builder, see [Reports](/en/concepts/reports).

## Exporting a Project Manifest

A project manifest packages your project's **map state and layer configuration** — basemap settings, layer styles, labels, legends, and visibility — into a single `manifest.json` file inside a zip archive. It's a lightweight way to back up or transfer how your project is styled and arranged.

**Steps:**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Project Settings">
    In your workspace, open the **Settings** panel (gear icon).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Export Manifest">
    Find the **Export Manifest** section and click the export button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download">
    Your browser downloads a `<project>-manifest.zip` containing `manifest.json`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Project manifest export is available on **Basic and above**.
