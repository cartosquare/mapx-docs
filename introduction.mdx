Traditional spatial analysis tools like ArcGIS and QGIS are powerful — but they demand weeks of training, expensive licences, and complex desktop installations just to answer a basic location question. MapX replaces that entire workflow with a browser-based AI chat interface: upload your data, type what you want to know, and get an interactive, shareable map in seconds. It is built for analysts, planners, and business teams who need GIS-grade answers without becoming GIS experts.

## Who Uses MapX

**Site selection analysts** use MapX to score candidate locations against catchment demographics, competitor proximity, and infrastructure access — work that previously required a dedicated GIS team.

**Real estate teams** overlay planning boundaries, transport links, and market data to qualify leads, build pitch decks, and answer client questions on the fly during meetings.

**Logistics planners** optimise delivery zones, flag coverage gaps, and model the impact of adding or removing depot locations without writing a single line of code.

**Energy and environmental consultants** combine satellite-derived land-use layers with asset registers and regulatory boundaries to run impact assessments and produce regulator-ready maps in minutes rather than days.
