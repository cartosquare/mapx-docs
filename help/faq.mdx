Get quick answers to the most common questions about MapX — from data safety and supported formats to plan limits, cancellation policies, and how MapX stacks up against traditional GIS tools.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to know GIS to use MapX?">
    No. MapX is designed for non-GIS users. Simply type your question or analysis request in plain English and MapX handles all the spatial analysis behind the scenes — no GIS training, specialist software, or technical knowledge required.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is my data safe?">
    Yes. Your data is stored securely in the cloud. Public projects are accessible by shareable link, while private projects (available on Pro and Enterprise plans) require authentication to view. MapX does not share your data with third parties.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What file formats does MapX support?">
    MapX supports **CSV**, **GeoJSON**, and **Excel** (`.xlsx` and `.xls`) files. After upload, MapX automatically detects latitude and longitude columns — no manual configuration needed in most cases.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can MapX do GIS-level analysis, not just visualization?">
    Yes. MapX performs full spatial operations, not just visual styling. Supported analyses include:

    - **Buffer** — generate distance zones around points, lines, or polygons
    - **Spatial join** — combine datasets based on geographic relationships
    - **Overlay** — intersect or union spatial layers
    - **Heatmap** — density-based visualization of point distributions
    - **Multi-criteria site scoring** — rank and identify optimal locations based on weighted spatial factors
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I cancel my plan anytime?">
    Yes. You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings — no need to contact support. After cancelling, you retain full access to your plan's features until the end of the current billing period.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if I outgrow my plan?">
    You can upgrade your plan at any time from your account settings. All your existing data and projects are fully preserved when you upgrade — nothing is lost during the transition.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can MapX generate professional reports?">
    Yes. MapX includes one-click HTML report generation. Reports automatically include maps, charts, data tables, and AI-written insights summarising your analysis. White-label reports (with your own branding) are available on the Enterprise plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does MapX compare to ArcGIS?">
    ArcGIS costs **$600+ per user per year** and requires dedicated GIS training to use effectively. MapX Pro costs **$49/month**, runs entirely in the browser, and requires no prior training or software installation. For the vast majority of business spatial analysis tasks — site selection, territory mapping, proximity analysis, and reporting — MapX is faster to get started and significantly more accessible.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the AI prompt limit?">
    Prompt limits depend on your plan:

    | Plan       | Monthly Prompt Limit |
    |------------|----------------------|
    | Free       | 50 prompts           |
    | Basic      | 500 prompts          |
    | Pro        | Unlimited            |
    | Enterprise | Unlimited            |

    Prompts reset automatically on your billing date, which you can view in your account settings.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does MapX work for large datasets?">
    Yes. MapX uses **GPU-accelerated WebGL rendering** to handle large volumes of spatial data smoothly. You can visualise and analyse datasets with **100,000+ data points at 60fps** without lag or degraded performance.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
