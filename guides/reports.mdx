When your analysis is complete, the last thing you want to do is spend an hour copy-pasting map screenshots into a slide deck. MapX reports package your entire analysis — maps, charts, data, and narrative — into a polished, professional HTML deliverable in a single click. No design tools, no formatting, no manual assembly. Just click **Export Report** and share.

## What's in a MapX Report

Every MapX report is self-contained and ready to share. Depending on the sections you choose to include, a report can contain:

- **Interactive or static maps** — the exact map views and layers from your analysis session, either as live interactive WebGL maps or high-resolution static exports
- **Charts** — automatically generated bar charts, histograms, scatter plots, and trend lines derived from your dataset and analysis results
- **Data summary tables** — filtered, ranked, or aggregated tabular data with column headers and formatting applied
- **AI-generated narrative insights** — a plain-English written summary of what MapX found in your data, structured as an executive summary with key findings and recommendations
- **Project branding** — your project name, and on Pro/Enterprise plans, your logo and color scheme

## Generating a Report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete your analysis in the chat">
    Run your spatial analysis to the point where the results are ready to present — whether that's a scored site selection layer, a logistics coverage gap study, a property heatmap, or any other output. MapX captures all layers and chat context as the source material for the report.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click Export Report in the project toolbar">
    Find the **Export Report** button in the top-right project toolbar (the icon looks like a document with a download arrow). Click it to open the report builder panel.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the sections to include">
    The report builder shows a checklist of available sections based on what your project contains:

    - ☑ Maps (select which layers and views to include)
    - ☑ Charts (select which charts to include)
    - ☑ Data Tables (choose filtered or full datasets)
    - ☑ AI Insights (include the AI-generated narrative summary)

    Toggle sections on or off, reorder them by dragging, and choose between interactive and static map rendering.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Preview the HTML report">
    Click **Preview** to see the report exactly as recipients will see it. The preview opens in a new browser tab and is fully interactive — you can scroll, click map layers, and verify the content before sharing.

    If something needs adjusting — a different map view, a missing table, a revised chart — close the preview, make changes in the main canvas, and re-open the report builder.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Download or share via link">
    When you're satisfied with the preview, you have two delivery options:

    - **Download HTML** — saves a self-contained `.html` file you can attach to an email or host anywhere
    - **Share via Link** — generates a public or password-protected URL that recipients can open in any browser, no MapX account required

    Shared report links remain active as long as your project exists. You can revoke a link at any time from the project settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Scheduled Reports

<Note>
  Scheduled reports are an **Enterprise-only feature**.
</Note>

Enterprise workspaces can configure reports to generate and deliver automatically on a recurring schedule — daily, weekly, monthly, or on a custom cron interval. Scheduled reports are useful for:

- **Executive dashboards** — send a weekly coverage or performance summary to stakeholders automatically
- **Client retainers** — deliver monthly market analysis reports to clients without manual effort
- **Operational monitoring** — flag logistics coverage changes or anomalies as soon as new data is uploaded

To set up a scheduled report, open **Project Settings → Scheduled Reports**, choose the report template, set the frequency and recipient email addresses, and save. MapX will regenerate the report using the latest project data and deliver it on schedule.
