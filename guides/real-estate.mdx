Real estate professionals use MapX to transform listing spreadsheets into compelling spatial stories — mapping properties, overlaying neighborhood context, comparing demographics across areas, and delivering polished client presentations. Whether you're a buyer's agent, a developer running feasibility studies, or a portfolio manager tracking market trends, MapX gives you GIS-grade analysis through a simple chat interface.

## Mapping and Analyzing Properties

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload your property listings CSV">
    Prepare a CSV with your listings data. MapX works with either address strings or explicit coordinates:

    - Address-based: include a column like `address` or `full_address` — MapX will geocode automatically
    - Coordinate-based: include `lat` and `lng` columns for instant rendering

    Include attributes you'll want to filter or visualize: `price`, `bedrooms`, `sqft`, `property_type`, `neighborhood`, `days_on_market`, and so on.

    Upload via **Upload Data** or drag the file onto the canvas. MapX will detect and plot all listings within seconds.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Generate a price heatmap">
    Get an immediate read on where values are concentrated:

    ```text
    Create a price heatmap for these properties
    ```

    MapX generates a weighted heatmap using the `price` column as intensity. Hot zones indicate high-value clusters; cooler areas show where lower-priced inventory concentrates. You can follow up to adjust radius, switch color ramps, or normalize by square footage.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add neighborhood context layers">
    Layer in the amenities and services that drive buyer decisions:

    ```text
    Show schools within 1km of each property
    ```

    ```text
    Add parks and green spaces within 500m
    ```

    ```text
    Show flood zone boundaries overlaid on my listings
    ```

    MapX fetches or accepts uploaded reference datasets and performs the spatial join, annotating each property with proximity data you can then filter on.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Filter and compare specific segments">
    Drill into the inventory most relevant to your client:

    ```text
    Show only 3-bedroom properties priced under $800k, color by neighborhood
    ```

    MapX applies the filter and re-styles the layer in one step. You can stack multiple filters, switch the color dimension, or ask for a side-by-side comparison of two price brackets.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Export a client-ready report">
    When your analysis is complete, generate a professional deliverable:

    - Click **Export Report** in the project toolbar
    - Choose which sections to include: the price heatmap, filtered listings map, neighborhood comparison charts, and AI-written market summary
    - Download as HTML or share a direct link

    The report is ready to send to your client without any additional formatting. See [Generating Reports](/guides/reports) for full details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common Real Estate Prompts

These prompts cover the most frequent real estate use cases — substitute your own column names and criteria as needed:

```text
Create a price heatmap for these properties

Show the median price per neighborhood as a choropleth map

Which properties are within 500m of a school and priced under $600k?

Color listings by days_on_market — highlight anything over 90 days

Show me properties that overlap with the flood zone layer

Compare average price per sqft across these five ZIP codes

Find the 10 highest-value properties and show their nearest transit stop

Show price trends over the last 12 months as a time-lapse animation
```

## Client Presentations

MapX reports are designed to go straight to clients — no extra formatting, no screenshot stitching, no slide-building. A single click on **Export Report** produces an HTML document containing:

- **Interactive or static maps** — the exact views from your analysis project
- **Charts** — price distributions, neighborhood comparisons, trend lines
- **Data tables** — filtered listing summaries with all key attributes
- **AI-generated narrative** — a plain-English market summary written by MapX based on what it found in your data

Reports are branded with your project name on all plans. On the **Pro plan**, you can add your own logo and choose custom accent colors. On **Enterprise**, full white-label is available — MapX branding is completely removed and replaced with your firm's identity, making reports indistinguishable from custom-built deliverables.

<Tip>
  For luxury and high-end property presentations, switch to a dark basemap before exporting your report. Dark basemaps make color-coded layers pop and give the output a premium, architectural feel that resonates with high-net-worth clients. Just ask: *"Switch to dark basemap"* before clicking Export Report.
</Tip>
